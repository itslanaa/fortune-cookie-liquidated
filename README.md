# Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader

One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated — the fortune that makes them laugh instead of cry.

> The market took your money, your sleep, and your dignity — but it left you this cookie. Eat it. HODL the crumbs.

Run with `node main.ts`; it writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), after checking the line is within the 1–160 character gate.
